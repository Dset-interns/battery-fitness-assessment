A4 height marker
  1. Print this page on A4 paper at 100% / Actual size (turn off "Fit to page", no headers or footers).
  2. Measure the black frame with a ruler: its outer edges must be 27.0 cm tall and 18.0 cm wide. If not, the printer scaled the page; print again at 100%.
  3. Stick the sheet flat on the wall beside the athlete, upright, with no creases or shadows across the frame. Keep other dark objects at least 5 cm away from the frame.
  4. Measure the height of the frame's top outer edge from the floor with a tape and enter it in the app.
