# Assessor's guide

For the person running the tests. The app works the same on an iPhone (added to the Home Screen from Safari), in an Android
browser, and as the Android app.

## Before the day

- Fully charge the phone. The endurance run is a long recording.
- Open the app once with internet so everything it needs is saved on the phone. The opening page says "Works offline" when it is ready.
- Put the phone on a tripod for the timed tests, the ball throw and the vertical jump. The phone must not move during a recording.
- Lay out what each test needs: tape on the floor, a wall scale, cones, a marker, a 400 m or 200 m loop. Use the checklists below.
- Choose Hindi or English at any time with the button at the top right.

## Running a session

1. **Login**: enter your name. It is printed on every report. There is no password.
2. **Register** each athlete: name, age, gender, school, programme ID if there is one. Age is taken now and does not change.
   Parental heights are optional.
3. **Consent**: the athlete signs on the screen. For anyone under 18 a parent or guardian reads and signs. An athlete without a
   signed consent cannot be assessed. The three first statements are required, the parental-height one is optional.
4. **Start assessment**: choose **Coach** (many athletes) or **Individual**, and **By test** (one station, everyone) or **By
   athlete** (everything for one athlete, then the next). Tick the athletes. You can switch order part-way.
5. **Session screen**: **Next up** is always at the top. Tap it, or any test in the list.
6. **Each test**: read the short instruction, tap **Start capture**, confirm the declaration (the athlete, or the parent or
   guardian, and you), set the lines if the test needs them, and record. The attempt is checked on the phone. If it is rejected, the
   reason is shown; fix that and record another attempt. The assessor decides whether to allow a retake.
7. **Report**: when you have at least one result, **View report** is at the bottom of the session. **Download PDF** opens the
   print screen: choose **Save as PDF**. **All athletes** makes one page each plus a roster page.
8. **Sync** (if your programme gave you a server address and a key): Login screen, **Server sync**, **Sync now**.

Height and weight are the only results you can type in. Weight needs a photo of the display.

## What each test needs

**1 Height.** Bare feet, heels, buttocks and upper back against the wall, feet together, arms by the sides, stretch up without
raising the heels. Phone about 2.5 m away at waist height. Use the saved wall reference photo or the sticky note.

**2 Weight.** Bare feet in the middle of the scale, still. Type the weight rounded to the nearest kg and photograph the display.

**3 Sit and Reach.** Floor tape from 0 to 53 cm between the legs, the 23 cm mark exactly on the heel line, taped down. Legs straight
in a V, heels about 30 cm apart, knees flat, toes up. Palms down, hands together, reach slowly and hold the farthest point for 2
seconds. No bouncing. Three attempts. Film from the side.

**4 Standing vertical jump.** Stand side-on to the wall scale. First raise the arm nearest the wall fully, heels down, and hold it for
1 second: that is the standing reach. Then lower the arm and jump straight up from the same spot, no steps or shuffling, and reach as
high as you can. The score is the jump reach minus the standing reach. Three attempts.

**5 Standing broad jump.** Behind the line, feet slightly apart, two-foot take-off and landing, no falling backward. Measured from
the line to the back of the heels. Three attempts. Film from the side.

**6 Backward overhead medicine ball throw.** Ball 2 kg for males, 1 kg for females. Tripod to the side of the lane, in landscape, seeing
the line, the whole flight and the landing. Tape along the lane with a marker at a known distance. In the app tap **Set throw line**
then the line on the ground in the video, **Set marker** then the marker, and choose its distance. The athlete stands with the heels at
the line and the back to the throw. Yellow boxes show what the camera tracks as the ball; the ball must stand out from the ground and
sky and nobody else should move in the lane. The ball should land along the tape. Three attempts.

**7 30 m run.** Tripod at the side in landscape, both lines in view, 30 m apart. Tap **Set start line** and **Set finish line**. One
athlete at a time, standing still behind the start line. Start recording, then "ready, go". Two trials.

**8 4 × 10 m shuttle run.** Lines 10 m apart, both in view. Tap **Set start line** and **Set far line**. One athlete at a time:
run to the far line, touch it, return and touch the start line, repeat, and on the fourth length cross the start line (40 m in all).
One attempt. The result is always amber because a hand touching the line cannot be seen.

**9 Sit-ups.** Side view. On the back, knees bent under 90°, heels within 30 cm of the buttocks, fingers clasped behind the neck,
elbows on the floor, feet held. Curl up until the elbows touch the knees and return with the elbows flat before the next one. 45
seconds. A sit-up does not count if the fingers come apart (2a), an elbow pushes off the floor (2b) or the elbows do not return flat (2c).

**10 800 m endurance run.** Tripod beside the start and finish line, in landscape, the line in view. Tap **Set start / finish line**
and choose 400 m laps (2 laps) or 200 m laps (4 laps). One athlete at a time, standing still behind the line. The recording stops by
itself after the finish, or 17 minutes after it starts. One attempt.

## When an attempt is rejected

The screen says why. The common reasons: the phone moved (put it on a tripod), no one or more than one person in view, the start or
finish was not seen (keep the lines in view and record until the athlete is past them), the athlete did not hold the standing reach,
or a rule was broken. Nothing is ever recorded as zero.

## Colours on the report

Green: measured against a reference in the picture. Amber: an estimate, or a measurement with a known limit; the page says which.
Red or the cross: rejected, with the reason. No grades or percentiles are given because there are no validated norms.
